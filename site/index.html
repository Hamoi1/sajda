A countdown above every window

The widget stays on top while you work. Pick one of six themes and four sizes, drag it anywhere, lock it in place, fade its background.

Azkar and tasbih, counted for you

≈3min

Matches the official Kurdistan timetable

Checked against a full year of published mosque tables, the average difference is about three minutes. Eleven more methods are built in, from Umm al-Qura to ISNA, and each prayer can be nudged to match your mosque.

A surah before you scroll on

At the times you choose, a mushaf page opens above everything and reveals the surah ayah by ayah. It closes once the last ayah is read. The whole Quran is bundled, so it works offline.
